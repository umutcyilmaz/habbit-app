# Copy Guidelines

Bloom copy should feel calm, private, mature, non-judgmental, discreet, and supportive. It should sound like a product that respects adult users, not a program that diagnoses, grades, or watches them.

## Approved Terms

Use terms like:

- pattern
- moment
- loop
- sensitive window
- gentle support
- pause
- reflection
- awareness
- optional
- you remain in control
- no judgment
- mindful
- intentional
- calmer routine
- notice
- choose

## Banned Terms

Avoid terms like:

- relapse
- addiction
- failure
- high-risk
- diagnosis
- treatment
- cure
- blocker
- surveillance
- monitoring
- performance score
- streak failure

These terms may appear in internal documentation only when identifying what Bloom should avoid.

## Privacy Copy Rules

- Be clear without overclaiming security.
- Prefer "local-first" over absolute claims.
- Explain what data is stored and why.
- Make deletion easy to find.
- Do not imply hidden tracking.
- Do not use sensitive wording in notifications.
- Avoid saying "anonymous" unless the implementation truly supports that claim.

Good:

- "Your reflections stay on this device unless you choose otherwise."
- "You can delete your Bloom data from Data Controls."
- "Notifications use discreet wording."

Bad:

- "Your data is completely secure forever."
- "We monitor your behavior to keep you accountable."
- "Your addiction data is anonymous."

## Protection Copy Rules

Protection is gentle support, not blocking.

Good:

- "Add support around a sensitive window."
- "Pause before continuing."
- "You can edit or turn this off anytime."

Bad:

- "Block yourself from failing."
- "Stop high-risk behavior."
- "We will prevent relapse."

## Progress Copy Rules

Progress is awareness, not abstinence.

Good:

- "You noticed boredom most often in the evening."
- "Pauses were more common before intentional choices this week."
- "A calmer next focus could be an evening pause."

Bad:

- "You failed twice this week."
- "Your performance score dropped."
- "Reset your streak."

## Paywall Copy Rules

Privacy, deletion, safety, and basic controls must never be paywalled.

Good:

- "Bloom Plus can add deeper pattern views and custom routines."
- "Core privacy and deletion controls are always available."

Bad:

- "Upgrade to delete your data."
- "Pay to unlock privacy."
- "Subscribe to stop failing."

## Notification Copy Rules

Notifications should be discreet and safe on a lock screen.

Good:

- "A gentle pause is ready."
- "Check in when you have a moment."
- "Your evening support is available."

Bad:

- "Porn craving detected."
- "You are in a high-risk window."
- "Do not relapse tonight."

## Good Copy Examples

- "What is present right now?"
- "Take 90 seconds before choosing your next step."
- "Saved. You can review patterns when you are ready."
- "This is optional. You remain in control."
- "No judgment. Just a moment to notice."
- "Evenings may be a sensitive window for you."

## Bad Copy Examples

- "You failed again."
- "Your addiction is getting worse."
- "We blocked your behavior."
- "Complete treatment to cure the problem."
- "Your performance score is low."
- "This is a dangerous high-risk period."

## Voice Checklist

Before shipping copy, ask:

- Does it avoid shame?
- Does it preserve user control?
- Does it avoid diagnosis and treatment language?
- Is it discreet enough for a shared space or lock screen?
- Does it describe patterns rather than judging behavior?
- Is the action optional where appropriate?
